## 1. Scientific target and contribution

Question: How do independently documented source-value, repeated-package and country-role discrepancies propagate into a public archive's four-group recipient-income allocation and its exact provider-country composition/within-provider accounting?

The contribution is an auditable mapping from documentary evidence to a complete finite sensitivity set, then to conclusions that do or do not survive that set. It is not a causal explanation, significance test, estimate of global export-credit exposure, representative error-rate study or correction of the predecessor's restricted TXF primary dataset. This is a reconciled public-archive extension. Separate replication of earlier published descriptive calculations from the present amendment. Do not imply that findings in the public OCI file invalidate results obtained from a different restricted source.

Keep source heterogeneity in the title, abstract and estimand. Even after the proposed changes, unreviewed amounts remain archive-reported values whose loan/guarantee/contract/aggregate bases can differ. No scenario certifies the remaining portfolio as non-overlapping principal or exposure. Project access, beneficiary welfare and institution behavior are not identified.

## 6. Exact four-class provider-country accounting

For each scenario, geography panel and class k, construct provider shares w_pt of all classified amounts and within-provider income shares h_pkt. For common positive-dollar providers, composition C_k = sum_p mean(h_pk) × change(w_p); within W_k = sum_p mean(w_p) × change(h_pk). Entry E_k = sum entering w_p,late h_pk,late; exit X_k = −sum exiting w_p,early h_pk,early. Use full classified-window denominators, not a renormalized balanced subset. Delta_k = 100(C_k+W_k+E_k+X_k). Retain separate entry and exit terms and common-support counts and volume shares.

Compute the same accounting for all nine adjacent annual pairs, not only endpoint pools. These annual identities display timing and lumpiness; they are not independent replicates or a time-series model. Preserve undefined pairs. Every income category and every provider contribution remains in the output, including zero contributions and negative values. Report all four terms with the headline share change, not a favored composition/within ratio. Ratios of a component to near-zero total change are not primary outputs.

## 7. Source-error propagation and finite-scenario envelopes

For each target (year/window/class/geography) and every accounting component, report minimum and maximum over all 24 scenarios, exact scenario IDs attaining each extremum, undefined-case count, and all underlying results. Describe these as finite-scenario envelopes conditional on the enumerated documentary treatments. They are not confidence intervals, global error bounds, nor identification regions for unknown actual exposure. Endpoint bounds on allocation do not automatically bound composition or within-provider terms; recompute each entire identity per case.

Report a sign as scenario-stable only if all 24 applicable defined cases have that sign, and disclose undefined cases. Treat numerical magnitude below 1e-10 percentage points as zero solely for sign reporting. A ranking is stable only if the same weak ordering holds in every case; report ties. Never infer robustness to unmodelled errors.

For each binary factor, calculate its paired marginal change at every fixed combination of the other factors. For D, calculate D1−D0, D2−D0 and D2−D1 at every A/O/G background. Report the full ranges and table of these context-specific effects on allocation and all four accounting terms. Do not present a single selected background as the general effect.

Quantify non-additive propagation using exact anchored finite-difference decomposition on each of two four-factor binary cubes: D0 versus D1, and D0 versus D2, crossed with A/O/G. For every nonempty subset of factors, use the alternating sum over its corners with other factors at archive level. Publish all first-, second-, third- and fourth-order terms; their sum must equal the fully changed corner minus the archive corner for each outcome. These are arithmetic interactions of source operations, not causal interactions. No significance tests or Shapley attribution are needed.

## 9. Deterministic influence diagnostics, not a model search

For every one of the 24 primary scenarios, delete each included original Renewable record in turn, with all original source rows represented in a ledger (excluded rows receive status 'already absent'). Recompute four-class endpoint changes and all four provider-country components for the international reporting portfolio, country-assigned international subset and all locations. Also compute the affected annual-share vector over all ten years. Source rows outside endpoint windows must correctly have zero endpoint influence, rather than be omitted from the annual diagnostic. Influence = outcome with deletion minus outcome without deletion. Missing/zero-amount records have zero dollar influence unless denominator/support definitions expose a programming error.

Repeat deleting each provider-country's full Renewable reporting portfolio, one provider at a time. Provider deletion changes the reporting population and is a fragility diagnostic, not estimation on a preferred balanced sample. Do not infer independent observations, standard errors or project counts. No record/provider is removed from the preferred results because it has high influence.

Rank record influence by maximum absolute change among the four income-class endpoint changes; separately rank each component by the same four-class maximum. Rank annual-share influence by maximum absolute change over all four classes and all ten years. Break ties by immutable row ID ascending; providers by stable provider label. Deposit every influence, not only the top entries. For each ranking report top-one and top-five shares of summed absolute influence; undefined if total absolute influence is zero. These are influence-concentration diagnostics, not financial-concentration shares. Also retain top-one/top-five recorded-value shares and record IDs in each endpoint as a distinct measure. Source splitting can change record-level rankings; provider-level diagnostics help reveal that limitation but do not cure it.

A leave-one-package-out secondary check is required for the two known multirow operations (Standard Bank1907/1908 and Karuma109/2253) and the three-row Dogger block, with fixed membership regardless of scenario; do not invent additional project clusters. These checks clarify why a single-record diagnostic can miss known linked records. No post-hoc trimmed estimator, preferred exclusion or p-value follows.

## 13. Conditional regional-income completion: reviewed mathematical addendum

The affine/vertex argument was verified before vertex computations. Apply this addition only to the primary fixed FY2013 classification, without further expansion. It supplements, rather than relabels, the finite 24-case classified-income envelopes.

For each of the 12 G1 core scenarios, and each of the six G1 Karuma agreement overlays, complete only the Standard Bank regional income allocation. Let U be its total scenario-specific observed support value (the sum of its loan and guarantee components), with Japan as known provider and archive FY2014 fixed. Let x=(x_L,x_LM,x_UM,x_H), each nonnegative and summing to U. Other known-income values are fixed; all other unknown-income amounts remain excluded from this targeted-completion denominator and reported separately. Work with all locations and the international reporting portfolio, not the country-assigned subset. Within the respective scope, denominator V_t equals known-income amounts other than Standard Bank plus U in the affected year/window. This new denominator is fixed across x, but differs from the uncompleted G1 classified denominator. Thus this is a separately labelled targeted-completion estimand.

Assumption: absent documented ultimate-country shares or income restrictions, permit any division of the regional amount among the four fixed income categories. This is an explicit unconstrained four-category allocation model; it does not establish actual deployment, disbursement, geographical eligibility of every specific country, or homogeneous support concepts. If source restrictions later preclude a category, amend the feasible simplex before computation; never tighten it based on desired results. The exact extrema are sharp for this stipulated completion model. Call them unrestricted-income outer envelopes: actual eligible Sub-Saharan destinations have not been exhaustively established, so these are not claimed to be sharp bounds on actual regional allocation.

Proof: each provider's total amount P_pt and portfolio total V_t are constant across x, so w_pt=P_pt/V_t and the set of common/entering/exiting positive-dollar providers are constant. Each within-provider class share h_pkt=(B_pkt+x_pkt)/P_pt is affine in x; here x is nonzero only for Japan in the affected period. Consequently endpoint shares, endpoint differences, common-provider composition and within terms, and entry/exit terms are all affine functions of x. Every allocation x is a convex combination of the four simplex vertices Ue_L, Ue_LM, Ue_UM, Ue_H. Therefore each scalar outcome attains its exact minimum and maximum at a vertex. This is analytic finite optimization, not a Monte Carlo approximation or probability interval.

For example, if Japan has positive support in both endpoint windows and the regional amount is in the early window, the derivative of composition with respect to x_k is (w_J,late−w_J,early)/(2P_J,early), and that of within is −(w_J,late+w_J,early)/(2P_J,early). Their sum is −1/V_early, matching the derivative of the class-share change; entering/exiting cases remain affine by their definitions. Use fractions here and multiply by100 for percentage points.

Compute all four vertices for all four income classes, every annual share, all nine adjacent-year and pooled-window changes and each provider decomposition term. Save numerator/denominator and support for every vertex. Report each scalar bound and its attaining vertex, plus the four full output vectors: the joint attainable set is their convex hull, so marginal extremes across classes/components generally cannot occur simultaneously. Across distinct A/O/D scenarios or timing overlays, preserve a union of these scenario-specific hulls; taking a convex hull across source scenarios would introduce unspecified mixtures. A union's scalar extremes can be summarized with both scenario and vertex IDs.

Validate constant denominators and support across vertices, simplex closure, decomposition closure, and the symbolic derivative identity in common-support cases. Independently evaluate a fixed interior check x=U/4 in each category and assert its outputs equal the arithmetic mean of the four vertex outputs to tolerance; it is a verification point, not an additional chosen scenario. Four-group shares must sum to one and four-group changes to zero in each completed target.

Show these targeted regional-completion bounds beside the uncompleted classified-share and finite-scenario envelopes with different labels. Bounds condition on the observed amount scenario, specified inclusion scope, fixed provider/FY, unrestricted four-category allocation and all other retained data. They do not bound missing-dollar records, other unresolved geography/income, unreviewed amount errors, finance principal, total worldwide international exposure or the published restricted-source study. No claim of global partial identification is authorized.
