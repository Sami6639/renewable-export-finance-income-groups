# OCI row 8129: bounded financial reconciliation

Reviewed 2026-10-18. Scope: existing frozen top-ten row only; no input, scenario, Word manuscript, or outcome edits. Original row: USD 1,720,000,000; FY2020; date 2020-12-18; China Eximbank; Souapiti (515MW) and Kaleta (240MW); IJGlobal transaction 54652. This review does not establish duplication or authorize a replacement value/year.

## Conclusion

**Amount corroborated as an approximately USD1.72bn aggregate CEXIM loan package, including development of Souapiti and restructuring of existing Kaleta; not demonstrated to be USD1.72bn of wholly incremental construction finance.** A first-party lender-counsel announcement documents successful closing, published 21 December 2020. The precise 18 December 2020 event date, component allocation, and identity of the earlier loan(s) within that package remain unverified. Official debt records independently establish a Souapiti loan signed in 2018 and disbursement in December 2020. These facts can coexist with a 2020 financial close: they do not justify moving the whole OCI row to 2018.

## Evidence ledger

### A. Lender's transaction counsel: aggregate finance amount and closing

Milbank, *Milbank Advises Export-Import Bank of China on $2.3 Billion Guinean Hydropower Projects*, 21 December 2020. First-party deal-participant announcement, not an official government debt record or signed contract.

https://www.milbank.com/en/news/milbank-advises-export-import-bank-of-china-on-dollar23-billion-guinean-hydropower-projects.html

Location: opening two substantive paragraphs; partner statements following deal-team paragraph. Reports financing provided to Guinea's Ministry of Finance and approximately USD1.72bn in CEXIM loans for Souapiti development plus restructuring of existing 240MW Kaleta. Souapiti is described as 450MW, unlike OCI's 515MW label. The USD2.3bn headline is the broader project/deal total, not the stated loan amount. Two partner comments explicitly describe closing; one uses “successfully closing this highly complex landmark financing.” This supports a completed package announced in 2020. It gives no exact closing day, underlying agreement dates, amount by component, or net-new/refinanced split. Do not treat its publication date as the contract date. Web-tool text was readable; direct HTML download returned 403, so no local full-page archive is claimed.

## Timeline and limits

- 4 September 2018: Souapiti agreement, approximately USD1.175bn; government-debtor evidence above.
- 30 June 2020: official bulletin carries the agreement among signed but undisbursed projects.
- December 2020: official sources report first Souapiti disbursement of approximately USD575m.
- 18 December 2020: OCI/IJGlobal row date, not independently verified here.
- 21 December 2020: Milbank reports completed approximately USD1.72bn multi-project financing, with Kaleta restructuring expressly included.

The exact facility bridge is unresolved. No signed combined-facility contract was obtained. There is no verified allocation of the USD1.72bn between Souapiti and Kaleta, or between new money, outstanding principal, refinancing, restructuring and any equity-acquisition debt. A residual calculated from USD1.72bn minus the earlier Souapiti loan would not establish the Kaleta financing amount. AidData project 187 and linked Kaleta project 53003 were used as discovery leads, not proof that the combined package is the sum of those records. No matching earlier OCI record was established, so there is no double-counting finding.
