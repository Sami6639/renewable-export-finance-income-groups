## Official 2014 evidence
Landing page: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/decreto-1091-2014-232342/texto
Official linked annex: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/232342_dec1091_pdf/archivo

Decree 1091/2014 is dated 17 July and published 18 July 2014. It approves a credit-agreement model for Argentina's Santa Cruz Kirchner/Cepernic project, with USD 4,714,350,000 commitment. The annex is a 325-page image PDF, downloaded in full. OCR was limited to PDF pages 1–32, covering definitions and insurance clauses; no claim is made to have read all 325 pages.

- PDF page 22, printed page 19: Sinosure Policy definition provides minimum 95% coverage of facility principal and interest, excluding the Relevant Interest Portion. Total Commitments on this same page are USD 4,714,350,000. Page image visually checked.
- PDF page 20, printed page 17: excluded Relevant Interest Portion is the first USD 300,000,000 of accruing interest, reduced as borrower interest payments are made until zero.
- PDF page 27, printed page 24, clause 4.1: insurance premium is separately defined, with a 7.1% ceiling against commitments plus estimated qualifying interest. Premium is not insured principal/exposure.

The model's policy definition concerns issued-or-to-be-issued cover; by itself it does not establish an issued policy face amount or exact effective date.

This is retrospective evidence in the official amendment model, not the standalone original insurance policy. It confirms 2014 policy timing, but not the row's exact 18 August date. Fiscal year 2014 is consistent with these records; the correct transaction-day convention is unresolved.

## Amount reconciliation and limits
The dataset amount equals 95% of the dataset-note's rounded USD 4.7bn debt figure. That arithmetic supports how the amount was derived; it does not prove the exact contractual exposure. Applying 95% to the official commitment produces USD 4,478,632,500, a principal-only arithmetic comparator, NOT a recommended replacement value or verified policy limit. Coverage also includes qualifying interest and is stated as a minimum percentage. The original issued policy schedule or insurer-confirmed insured amount was not obtained.

The USD 14.35m difference between rounded USD 4.7bn and USD 4.71435bn must not be characterized as fees: no inspected evidence establishes that explanation. The decree separately gives the original construction-price equivalent as USD 4,714,347,111, close to the exact facility total.
