# Standard Bank rows 1907/1908: primary-source reconciliation

Evidence reviewed 18 October 2026. No delivered data or manuscript edited. This supplement supersedes the earlier audit only on the financial evidence now recovered.

## Verified finding

The two archive rows refer to different instruments supporting one credit-line operation, not two independently identified projects. JBIC's own environmental brochure explicitly identifies an untied loan, total credit-line ceiling USD250 million, JBIC portion USD125 million, Mizuho as cofinancier/lead arranger, and a partial JBIC guarantee of the cofinanced portion. A later official JBIC Africa brochure reports USD237.5 million as combined loan/guarantee for the named operation signed 27 March 2015. This supports a derived guarantee component of USD112.5 million, conditional on the combined amount being additive principal commitments of the same operation. No retrieved document explicitly states a USD112.5m guarantee or its percentage.
