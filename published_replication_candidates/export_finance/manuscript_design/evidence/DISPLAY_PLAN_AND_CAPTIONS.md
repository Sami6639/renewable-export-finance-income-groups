## Variables and denominators

period: fiscal year or frozen pooled early/late window. scenario: Archive or Quarantine, the latter removes exactly the three existing source IDs. technology: existing audited Renewable or Fossil classification. income_class: H, UM, LM, L, or Unclassified, fixed FY2013. amount_usd: sum of available recorded amounts for that class and monetary basis. observed_usd_denominator: all observed amounts in the scope, including unclassified income. classified_usd_denominator: observed H+UM+LM+L amounts. amount_share_observed: class amount divided by observed denominator. amount_share_classified: class amount divided by classified denominator, undefined for Unclassified. entries: all class reporting entries, including zero and missing-dollar records. all_entries_denominator: all scoped reporting entries. classified_entries_denominator: scoped H+UM+LM+L entries. entry_share_all and entry_share_classified follow those respective denominators; the latter is undefined for Unclassified. missing_amount_entries: records lacking the selected monetary amount, not missing-income records. amount_income_coverage: classified observed amount / all observed amount. entry_income_coverage: classified entries / all entries. high_share_classified: H / (H+UM+LM+L). non_high_usd: UM+LM+L amount. Zero denominators yield undefined values, never zero shares.
