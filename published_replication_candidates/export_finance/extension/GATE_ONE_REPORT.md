# OCI extension: measurement gate
18 October 2026. Reproducible script: `audit_extension.py`. The dated plan was saved before these summaries. This is an open-data extension of Censkowsky et al. (2025), DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-55981-0, using Oil Change International's Public Finance for Energy Database, archived in Zenodo 14261240.

## What is ready
The FY2013–2022 main scope contains 2,908 ECA financing entries, $526.976140 billion reported nominal USD and $549.922048 billion once-deflated 2020 USD. These are recorded financing commitments/volumes, not disbursements, new capital mobilized, expected fiscal expenditure or guarantee payouts. Loans, guarantees and mixed financing are separate reported instruments. The archive is not a complete FY2023 panel.

Sequential ECA exclusions: 12 electric-vehicle entries, one non-energy Climate entry, 62 Petrochemical entries and three BNDES/JICA development-finance entries. All 2,986 original ECA entries and exclusion reasons remain available. Eight entries with 2012 approval dates are retained because FY is in scope; 327 main entries differ between approval year and FY.

Main source amountUSD remains unchanged, including one missing value. The missing entry is Benarix Environmental Inc., EDC, FY2020, source Excel row8281. The source itself supplies CAD500,000 and XR0.71309, permitting a clearly separate $356,545 source-FX repair sensitivity. It is not included in main dollar totals; the entry remains in entry counts. Other observed USD values are never replaced by FX products. There are 586 ECA Original×XR discrepancies at tolerance max($0.01, one part per million); 34 retained observations differ by more than 1%. Some reflect rounding, others require source-level verification. Original and diagnostic values are preserved.

## Coverage and accounting
- 304 unknown/multiple/regional recipient entries contain $123.036775 billion, 23.35% of main nominal volume. They remain in portfolio totals and cannot be assigned recipient income from provider identity.
- 44 explicitly aggregate/annual-total entries contain $116.587965 billion. All are among unknown-location entries. The User Guide defines Canadian/Korean aggregates as annual institution/energy totals net of individually reported transactions. They are retained, not dropped or duplicated across recipients. Some annual-total records are assigned individual instrument labels in the source; preserve these labels and the aggregate flag rather than assuming all have an identifiable loan/guarantee split.
- 16 subsequent exact duplicate copies involve 32 rows and $0.332784 billion (0.06315%). Main retains them because identical records alone do not establish source double-counting; a mechanical sensitivity removing only subsequent full-row copies contains 2,892 entries. Never deduplicate project names.
- One recorded zero-volume observation remains in entry counts, with zero dollar weight. No negative nominal amounts occur in the main sample. Do not treat a zero entry as positive dollar common support.
- Provider, fiscal-year, technology and instrument group totals reconcile to the same main dollar total. Broad technology groups use the published fossil/renewable/grid rules with grid precedence. Renewable and OCI Clean are distinct definitions, since the paper includes hydro/biomass/biofuels in renewable.

## Currency and published-output reconstruction
The deposited IMF IFS workbook identifies US all-items CPI, downloaded 18 September 2024. Recomputing CPI/CPI2020 exactly matches its rebased row. Main real values divide nominal amountUSD by the FY-specific ratio exactly once. Nominal values remain the benchmark.

A separate diagnostic emulates the deposited Element3 and Element5 code using approval year, the source's row-index handling and exclusion of Canada, solely to reconstruct Supplementary Figure1 OCI series. Across all 33 year/energy cells for 2013–2023, the deposited twice-deflation path matches published source values within $2,222 maximum (about 0.000002222 billion), consistent with numeric precision. The single-deflation diagnostic differs. This is evidence that the deposited supplementary OCI aggregate series follows that path. It is not a claim about the paper's proprietary TXF main estimates or their substantive conclusions. Original Stata was not run; the transparent reconstruction is in Python and independently checked.

## Required resolution before income comparisons
The raw recipient codes contain conflicting name/code combinations, including China/CH, Bangladesh/BG and Occupied Palestinian Territories/IL. Do not blindly join CC. Name-validated recipient mapping is required. Unknown geographic location and known location lacking an income mapping must be separate flags.

The archived World Bank workbook has a 19 February 2024 filename and notes specifying 2022-GNI thresholds, but also a stale July2022–July2023 sentence. Until independently verified, describe it as the fixed archived classification rather than claiming a definite historical vintage. Historical-income robustness is not available from this file alone.

Next proposed comparison, subject to review: fixed archived income classification; equal pooled FY2013–2015 and FY2020–2022 windows; exact joint provider×technology×instrument common-support composition/within-cell decomposition plus explicit entry/exit terms. Use full-period denominators, show allocation bounds for unknown volume, and report loan, guarantee and mixed/aggregate portfolios separately. Do not attempt separate provider/technology/instrument Shapley attribution in the first pass. No income-outcome comparison or regression has been run at this gate.

### Classification resolution after measurement review
The historical World Bank resource DR0095334 was independently verified and replaces the ambiguous archived-package classification in the proposed extension. `income_mapping_freeze.json` freezes FY13 (AA, based on2011 GNI) as principal and FY23 (AK, based on2021 GNI) as sensitivity, each held fixed over the full financing period. All known recipient labels map by exact country names or explicit aliases. Three retained entries have raw CC/name conflicts (Palestinian territories/IL and Bangladesh/BG); name-based mapping resolves these without altering raw fields. The China/CH conflict lies outside the main energy scope. FY13 has no unmapped known geography. FY23 has one known-geography entry in Venezuela whose income group is missing, explicitly retained as unclassified. This mapping preparation does not compute income outcomes.
